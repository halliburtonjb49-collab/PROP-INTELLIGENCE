## 3. App purpose, audience, and value

PI Prop Intelligence is an independent sports research and market-intelligence application for adults who want to organize and compare sports-proposition data. It consolidates available market information by sport, provider, player, and category; presents research signals and PI Trust indicators; provides scoreboards and market context; and lets users save and track research selections.

The app does not accept wagers, process bets, hold funds, pay winnings, or act as a sportsbook. Provider names identify the source of market data. Tracked selections are research records only.

## 4. Accessing the app and principal features

Apple reviewers may use the demo-account credentials entered in the App Review Information section of App Store Connect.

After signing in:

1. Open **Props** or **Market Board**.
2. Select a sport from the sports navigation.
3. Select a data provider and category, or use Filters.
4. Open a prop card to review PI Trust and research details.
5. Select Over or Under to add or remove a tracked research selection.
6. Open **Slip/Watch** to review live tracking and completed history.
7. Open **ML Games/Scoreboard** for current game information.
8. Open **Menu > Account** to manage or delete the account.
9. Open **Prop Chat** to view community rooms. Message actions provide report and block controls.

Paid access is available from the account/membership area. The purchase screen displays each subscription's title, duration, current App Store price, restore-purchases control, Terms of Use, and Privacy Policy.

## 5. External services

PI Prop Intelligence uses the following services:

- Supabase for authentication, account data, and community data.
- Apple and Google for optional federated sign-in.
- RevenueCat and Apple In-App Purchase for subscription entitlement management.
- OneSignal for optional notifications.
- Sports data providers, including SportsGameOdds and API-Sports, for sports schedules, scores, and market data.
- Render for protected backend API services.
- Vercel for web application and public-site delivery.

No external service processes wagers through the app.

## 6. Regional availability

The app provides the same research functionality in its supported regions. Available sports, events, providers, and markets may vary based on the live inventory supplied by external data providers. This is an inventory difference, not a regional wagering feature.

## 7. Regulated activity and third-party material

PI Prop Intelligence is a sports-data research product and is not a gambling operator or sportsbook. It does not facilitate transactions, deposits, withdrawals, wagers, or prizes. Third-party provider and league names are used descriptively to identify sports and the source of market data. Data is obtained through configured data-provider services and APIs.

## 8. In-App Purchase

Users can purchase an auto-renewable subscription that unlocks the corresponding Core or Pro research features. To reach the purchase screen, sign in and open the account or membership area. The screen displays the subscription name, duration, price, trial eligibility when supplied by Apple, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Restore Purchases.

Subscriptions change access to research features only. They do not provide wagering funds, credits, prizes, or gambling functionality.
